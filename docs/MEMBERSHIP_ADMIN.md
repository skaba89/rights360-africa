# Membership administration
Institutional administrators with MFA may list memberships of their own organization and change the role or active flag of non-admin collaborators. The target organization is derived from the authenticated session, never from the request. Self-modification, promotion to administrator, modification of existing administrators and platform-granted accounts are denied. Every change revokes the target's sessions in that organization and records a tenant audit event. Other organizations and global account status remain unaffected. This phase manages existing memberships only; collaborator invitations, administrator succession, granular role customization, audit viewing and independent privileged SQL review remain outstanding.
