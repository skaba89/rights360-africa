# Work registry
Institutional work records support music, literary, audiovisual, visual and other categories, a unique internal reference, optional musical ISWC (format and checksum validation, not external verification), a principal artist in the same organization, and draft/registered/archived status. A composite foreign key prevents cross-organization artist references, and forced RLS scopes work rows. Each endpoint checks the effective works entitlement and RBAC. Admins and managers write; auditors read. The principal artist is a catalog association, not proof of legal ownership. The portal supports searching, filtering, pagination and editing; the artist UUID is entered manually. Multi-party rights and shares, versioned declarations, identifiers from external registries, attachments, approval workflows and browser E2E remain outstanding. No production deployment.
