# Tariffs and royalty estimates
Institutional administrators may create versioned tariffs for a category and usage type with explicit validity dates and currency. Supported methods are flat, revenue percentage (basis points), capacity and frequency (per supplied unit). Money uses integer minor units and half-up percentage rounding; no exchange conversion or floating-point arithmetic. Quotes require an explicitly selected tariff version and an active license with matching category, usage and date. Historical versions remain selectable; overlapping dates are possible and automatic version selection is deliberately not implemented. Revenue inputs must already be in the tariff currency. Quotes are estimates, not invoices or legal amounts due. No official tariffs are preloaded. Legal approval, tariff scope/territory, supporting usage evidence, automated selection, taxes, invoice persistence, payment and browser E2E remain outstanding. No production deployment.
