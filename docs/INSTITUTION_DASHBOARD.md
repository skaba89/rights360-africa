# Institutional dashboard
The authenticated portal offers an institution workspace with real organization identity, module grant/effective configuration, and administrator-only collaborator management. Administrators can enable entitled modules, change collaborator roles and access, issue and revoke invitations. Managers and auditors see module status; artists see organization identity only. Server-side permissions remain authoritative. Business modules are explicitly marked as not implemented. Invitation codes require secure manual delivery, and revocation requires the invitation UUID. No fake KPIs or customer data. Static wiring tests are included; browser E2E, invitation listing, SMTP, business modules and production review remain outstanding.
