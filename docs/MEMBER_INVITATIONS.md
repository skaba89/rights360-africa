# Collaborator invitations
Institutional administrators with MFA can invite new manager, auditor or artist accounts in their session organization. Tokens have 384 bits of entropy, expire after 24 hours, are stored only as digests and are consumed in the account creation transaction. A replacement invitation revokes prior pending invitations for the same email and organization. Explicit revocation is supported. Existing accounts cannot be linked or overwritten. No administrator or platform role may be assigned. Deliver tokens privately to verified recipients; SMTP, invitation listing, recipient verification, existing-account membership, retention and security review remain outstanding. Suspended organizations cannot accept invitations, but unexpired invitations may become valid again after reactivation. MFA is optional for non-admin roles under the current policy.
