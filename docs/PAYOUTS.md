# Payout preparation
An administrator or manager prepares one order per distribution and active beneficiary. Amount and currency are derived from posted distribution lines, never accepted from client input. Administrators and auditors may review; two distinct reviewers other than the creator are required. Rejection is terminal. Review decisions are append-only, and API checks plus a database trigger serialize reviews. The status approved_for_review_only does not authorize execution. No destination account or wallet data is collected, no provider is contacted, and execution_enabled is always false. Channels are labels, not integrations. Before actual payments, add dedicated finance reviewer roles (rather than auditor), verified receipts and legal rights approvals, independent MFA assurance, beneficiary verification, cancellation/reissue, provider contracts, secure destination management, idempotent provider execution, reconciliation and accounting settlement. Multi-user approval integration and browser E2E remain to be expanded. No production deployment.
