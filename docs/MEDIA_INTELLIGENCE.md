# Media Intelligence baseline
The media module records declared broadcasts against operators, with external references, timestamps, duration, title and artist metadata. Duplicate external references are prevented per operator. An optional SHA-256 digest may be supplied by the API; it is not an acoustic fingerprint and is not verified against audio. Candidates are exact case-insensitive title matches against registered musical works in the same tenant. Administrators may record a single human match/unmatched decision; this is not verified evidence of broadcast, infringement or royalty liability. RLS, composite foreign keys, role and module checks, audit, portal and regression tests apply. No audio capture, acoustic recognition, AI inference, stream monitoring, external providers, automatic billing or distribution. Future work: authorized audio ingestion, recording-level identifiers, real acoustic fingerprinting, evaluation of precision/recall, review correction/versioning, source evidence and browser E2E. No production deployment.
